Superfeet Green is one of the most recognizable insoles in running and hiking stores. It's firm, structured and has a deep heel cup. For some flat feet it's a game changer. For others it's too much. Here's how to tell which group you're in.

## What Superfeet Green is

Superfeet Green is a full-length insole with a rigid stabilizer cap under the heel and arch, a deep heel cup and a fairly thin foam top layer. It's designed for shoes with plenty of volume, such as running shoes, hiking boots and casual shoes with removable insoles.

## How it feels

**Firm.** This isn't a cushioned insole. The arch support is noticeable from the first step, and the heel cup cradles the back of the foot.

**Structured.** The stabilizer keeps the insole from flexing much through the middle, which is what gives it its supportive feel.

**An adjustment.** Most people need to break it in gradually. Start with an hour or two a day and build up over a week or two.

## Who it suits

- People with **flexible flat feet** who want more structure
- Runners and hikers who **overpronate** and want extra control in a shoe they already like
- Anyone who likes a **firm, supportive feel** underfoot
- People who wear **high-volume shoes and boots** with room for a thicker insole

## Who should look elsewhere

- People with **rigid flat feet**. A tall, firm arch can press painfully on a foot that can't flex into it.
- Anyone who wants **soft cushioning** rather than support.
- People with **low-volume or narrow shoes**, where the insole may crowd the foot.

If that sounds like you, consider a lower-profile or more cushioned option. See our guide to [orthotic insoles for flat feet](/orthotic-insoles-for-flat-feet-2026/).

## Other Superfeet options

Superfeet makes several variations, including thinner and lower-volume versions for tighter shoes and carbon-fiber versions for performance footwear.

![Superfeet Carbon insoles](/uploads/2022/06/Superfeet-Carbon-Shoe-Inserts-Thin-Orthotic-Inserts.jpg)

## Tips for using them

1. **Remove the stock insole** before putting Superfeet in.
2. **Trim to fit** using the original insole as a template, if needed.
3. **Break them in slowly.** Some arch soreness in the first days is common. Sharp pain is not.
4. **Replace them** when the top layer is worn through or the support feels noticeably weaker.

## The verdict

Superfeet Green is a solid choice if you have flexible flat feet and like firm support. If you have rigid arches or prefer cushioning, choose something gentler. And if pain persists, a podiatrist can tell you whether a custom orthotic would serve you better.
