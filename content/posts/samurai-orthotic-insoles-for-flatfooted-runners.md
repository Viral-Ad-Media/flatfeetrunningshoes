Samurai Insoles is a smaller brand that has built a following among people with flat feet and plantar fasciitis. Its insoles are known for firm, high arch support and a deep heel cup. Here's what to know before you buy.

![Samurai Insoles](/uploads/2022/06/SAMURAI-2.jpg)

## What makes Samurai different

Samurai designs its insoles around a **firm, contoured arch** that's meant to hold the foot in a more neutral position. Compared with many soft drugstore insoles, they feel structured and supportive rather than cushy. Several models are available, including options with extra padding and lower-profile versions for tighter shoes.

## The main models

### Samurai Ninjas

![Samurai Ninjas insoles](/uploads/2022/06/Samurai-Insoles-Ninjas-Plantar-Fasciitis-Relief-Arch-Support-Shoe-Insoles.jpg)

A versatile full-length insole with firm arch support. It's a good starting point for runners who want structure in their everyday trainers.

### Sumos by Samurai

![Sumos by Samurai insoles](/uploads/2022/06/Sumos-by-Samurai-Insoles.jpg)

A more padded version for people who want the same support with extra cushioning underfoot. It's useful for long days on your feet or heavier runners.

## Who Samurai insoles suit

- Runners with **flexible flat feet** who want firm, noticeable arch support
- People with **plantar fasciitis** or arch pain who've found soft insoles don't help
- Anyone who **overpronates** and wants to add control to a neutral shoe

## Who should be cautious

- People with **rigid flat feet** may find a tall, firm arch uncomfortable
- Runners in **low-volume racing shoes** may not have room for a structured insole
- Anyone with **diabetes, neuropathy or circulation problems** should check with a podiatrist before using firm orthotics

## Getting used to them

Firm orthotics need a break-in period. Wear them for an hour or two on the first day and add a little time each day. Use them for walking before running in them. Mild arch fatigue is normal at first. Sharp pain, numbness or blisters are signs to stop.

## Samurai vs Superfeet

Both brands offer firm, structured support. Samurai's arch is often described as higher and more pronounced, while [Superfeet Green](/superfeet-green-insoles-review-for-flat/) has a slightly more moderate profile with a very deep heel cup. If one feels too aggressive, the other may suit you better.

## The bottom line

Samurai Insoles are a strong choice for flatfooted runners who want firm, high arch support and haven't had luck with softer insoles. Start slowly, make sure your shoes have room, and see a podiatrist if your pain doesn't improve.
