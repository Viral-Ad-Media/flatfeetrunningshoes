Finding the right running shoe with flat feet can take some trial and error. This guide walks through the shoe lines that flat-footed runners return to year after year, and what each one does best. Brands update these models regularly, so think of them as families rather than one exact version.

## Before you choose

Ask yourself three questions:

1. **Do you overpronate?** If your ankles roll in noticeably, start with stability shoes. If not, a stable neutral shoe may be enough. Not sure? Read [flat feet vs overpronation](/flat-feet-vs-overpronation-differences/).
2. **How much cushioning do you like?** Some runners love a soft, high-stack ride. Others prefer to feel the ground.
3. **Do you need extra width?** See our [guide for flat and wide feet](/running-shoes-for-flat-feet-and-wide-feet-2026-guide/).

## Shoe lines to try

### Brooks Adrenaline GTS

![Brooks Adrenaline GTS](/uploads/2022/12/Brooks-Adrenaline-GTS-22.jpg)

A long-time favorite for flat feet. Its "GuideRails" support the heel and keep the knee tracking straight without feeling stiff. A dependable daily trainer for most mileage.

### Saucony Hurricane

![Saucony Hurricane](/uploads/2022/12/Saucony-Hurricane-23-Best-Cushioning.jpg)

Saucony's maximum-support shoe, with plenty of soft cushioning and a stable base. A good pick for heavier runners or long, easy miles.

### Hoka Gaviota and Arahi

![Hoka Arahi](/uploads/2022/12/Hoka-Arahi-6.jpg)

Hoka's stability shoes pair a wide, rockered platform with firmer foam on the sides to guide the foot. The Gaviota is the more cushioned of the two, and the Arahi is a little lighter.

### Brooks Launch GTS

![Brooks Launch GTS](/uploads/2022/12/Brooks-Launch-GTS-8.jpg)

A lighter, snappier stability shoe that suits tempo runs and runners who don't want a bulky feel.

### New Balance 860

![New Balance 860](/uploads/2022/12/New-Balance-Fresh-Foam-X-860v12.jpg)

A traditional stability trainer with firmer medial foam and a wide range of widths, making it a strong option for flat, wide feet.

### ASICS Gel-Kayano and GT series

![ASICS Gel-Kayano](/uploads/2022/12/ASICS-Gel-Kayano-28.jpg)

The Gel-Kayano is ASICS' premium stability shoe, with generous cushioning and a supportive heel. The GT-2000 is a lighter everyday option, and the GT-4000 has traditionally offered firmer support at a lower price.

## Fit checklist

- A thumb's width of space in front of your longest toe
- No heel slip when you jog
- No pressure on the top of the foot or the sides of the forefoot
- Arch support you can feel but that doesn't dig in
- Comfort from the first run, with no "breaking in" needed

## When shoes aren't enough

If you still have arch, heel or shin pain in a well-fitted stability shoe, try [orthotic insoles](/orthotic-insoles-for-flat-feet-2026/) and see a podiatrist or physiotherapist. Persistent pain is worth checking, not running through.

## The bottom line

There's no single best shoe for every flat foot. Start with one of the stability lines above, try on two or three, and pick the one that feels secure, roomy and comfortable from the first stride.
